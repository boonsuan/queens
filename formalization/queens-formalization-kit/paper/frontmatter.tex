\begin{abstract}
Place a queen in each successive column of an infinite $\N\times\N$ chessboard,
always choosing the lowest row such that no two queens may attack one another. 
We prove that the row~$q_n$ occupied by the queen in the $n$th column satisfies 
$q_n=n\phi+O(1)$ or $q_n=n/\phi+O(1)$, where $\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ is the golden ratio.
\end{abstract}
